All-natural hair clay made by a barber, for real hair.

## About
The Effect was created by Tim Medina, a barber who wanted a clay that gave real texture and hold without the junk. Every jar is made with natural ingredients.

## How to use
1. Start with dry or slightly damp hair.
2. Scoop a small amount and warm it between your palms.
3. Work it through from roots to ends.
4. Shape and style. Add more for extra hold.
