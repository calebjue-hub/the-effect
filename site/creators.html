Creator FAQ

Questions

How do I get paid?

Every creator gets a personal link and discount code. Sales through either are tracked automatically and paid out monthly.

Is there a follower minimum?

No. Smaller accounts often have the most engaged followers. We look at how real and consistent your content is, not the number.

Do I have to post a set amount?

No quotas. Creators who post regularly earn the most, and our most active creators get bonuses and free restocks.

I'm a barber. Can I sell it in my shop?

Yes. Apply and choose "I'm a barber or stylist," and we'll follow up about wholesale pricing.
