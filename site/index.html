Details

A styling paste with the grip and matte finish of a clay. Works on short to medium hair, straight to wavy. Restyle with your fingers through the day, and it rinses out with one wash.

How to use
  1. Start with dry or towel-dried hair.
  2. Warm a fingertip's worth between your palms.
  3. Rake it through from back to front, roots to ends.
  4. Twist and piece with your fingers. Add more for extra hold.
Full ingredients

Bentonite clay, kaolin clay, beeswax, shea butter, castor oil, jojoba oil, rosemary oil.

The formula

Not just natural.
Good for your hair.

"Natural" on a label doesn't say much. Every ingredient in Texture has a job: hold your style, or take care of the hair and scalp underneath it.

  1. 01Hold

    Bentonite clay

    The grip. Hold and a matte finish, and it soaks up excess oil so hair doesn't go flat by noon.

  2. 02Texture

    Kaolin clay

    A gentle, fine clay that builds texture and volume without drying out your scalp.

  3. 03Hold

    Beeswax

    Flexible hold that keeps its shape but still lets you restyle with your hands.

  4. 04Condition

    Shea butter

    Softens and conditions each strand, so hair feels like hair, not crunchy.

  5. 05Scalp

    Castor & jojoba

    Jojoba is close to your scalp's own oils, so it moisturizes without clogging. Castor adds strength and slip.

  6. 06Scalp

    Rosemary oil

    A barbershop favorite for scalp care, with a clean herbal scent instead of synthetic fragrance.

Made without

Our story

Made by the guy cutting your hair.

Effect started behind the chair. Tim Medina is a master barber who spent years reaching for products that either held but looked greasy, or looked right but fell flat by lunch, and too many of them listed ingredients he wouldn't want on his clients' scalps every day.

So he made his own. Texture is the paste he finishes his clients with: strong hold, matte finish, and a formula that takes care of your hair while it holds.

FAQ

Questions

What makes it different from other clays?

Most styling products only think about hold. Texture is made from ingredients that also condition your hair and scalp, and it's made without parabens, phthalates, petroleum or silicones.

Will it flake or build up on my scalp?

No. It's made to rinse out with one wash using regular shampoo, so nothing builds up on your scalp or turns into white flakes during the day.

Is it a clay, a paste or a pomade?

A styling paste with a clay-like matte finish: the grip of a clay, the easy spread of a paste, and none of the wet shine of a pomade.

What hair types does it work on?

Short to medium hair, straight to wavy. Great for textured crops, quiffs, messy fringes and side parts. Thick hair may need a little more.

How long does a jar last?

With daily use, most people get about two to three months out of a 100g jar.
