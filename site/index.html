How to use it

Thirty seconds, four steps. Less is more. You can always add.

  1. 01

    Dry hair

    Start with dry or towel-dried hair for the most grip.

  2. 02

    Warm it up

    Scoop a fingertip's worth and rub between your palms until it's clear.

  3. 03

    Work it in

    Rake it through from the back to the front, roots to ends.

  4. 04

    Shape it

    Twist, push and piece it with your fingers. Add more for extra hold.

Questions, answered

Is it a clay, a paste or a pomade?

Texture is a styling paste with a clay-like matte finish. It gives the grip and texture of a clay with the easy spread of a paste, and none of the wet shine of a pomade.

What hair types does it work on?

Short to medium-length hair, straight to wavy. It's great for textured crops, quiffs, messy fringes and side parts. Thick hair may need a little more product.

How much should I use?

Start with a fingertip-sized scoop. Warm it fully in your palms before applying. Add more only if you need extra hold.

Does it wash out?

Yes. One wash with regular shampoo takes it out.

How long does a jar last?

With daily use, most people get about two to three months from a 100g jar.
